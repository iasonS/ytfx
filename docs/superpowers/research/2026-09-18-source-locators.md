# MH Stats source locator cards (research, 2026-09-18)

Produced by a 15-agent survey; every card was then re-tested by a skeptic agent against a
held-out monster, and the `VERDICT` / `FAILED` lines record what actually broke. Treat the
FAILED entries as mandatory: they are locators that looked right and were not.

Downloads referenced here are cached under `~/tmp/mhstats-cache/` on the author's machine;
`tools/mhstats/lib/fetch.js` re-downloads anything missing.

    locator: robomeche: root["Monster Data"][gameId].AngryTable[0].Upper.Limit (.Lower.Limit for Low Rank) (mhdb: absent)

    locator: robomeche: root["Monster Data"][gameId].AngryTable[0].Upper.Time (.Lower.Time for Low Rank) (mhdb: absent)

  - hitzones present=True unit=robomeche: percent (0-100 int); mhdb: fraction (0.0-1.0 float)
    locator: robomeche (preferred): root["Monster Data"][gameId].Hitzones.Rows[j] with .Part (display name, e.g. "Head"), .State ("" = default state; "Weak", "State 1", "Break*", "AT Wnd", ... = conditional), .Meat.Slash / .Meat.Blow / .Meat.Shot (ints 0-100; also Fire/Water/Thunder/Ice/Dragon/Stun). HEAD = Rows.find(r => r.Part==="Head" && r.State==="").Meat. MAX RAW (default state) = max over Rows.filter(r => r.State==="" && !/^(HIDE|Weak Point|Unmatched)/.test(r.Part)) of max(Meat.Slash, Meat.Blow, Meat.Shot). mhdb (alternative): monsters[i].parts[k].kind (slug: "head", "torso", "left-wing" ...) and .multipliers.slash / .blunt / .pierce (floats 0-1 == robomeche Meat/100; verified Rathalos head 0.65/0.7/0.6 vs 65/70/60).
    notes: mhdb parts carry NO state flag and mix in 'hide' parts and conditional weak spots, so a naive max over mhdb parts returns 1.0 for Xu Wu and Omega Planetes; robomeche's State column lets you restrict to the default state (max_base vs max_all differ for 23 of 34 monsters, e.g. Doshaguma 54 vs 80).   - head_stagger present=True unit=damage points (base, before quest flinch modifiers / player scaling)
